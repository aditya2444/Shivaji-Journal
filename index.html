About Shivraj 350

Shivraj 350: Multidisciplinary Journal is a peer-reviewed, academic platform dedicated to fostering interdisciplinary research and dialogue across the sciences, social sciences, humanities, and professional studies.

The journal aims to promote original thinking, critical inquiry and innovative solutions to contemporary challenges with global relevance and local impact.

Published by Shivaji College, University of Delhi, this landmark edition celebrates 350 Years of Hindavi Swaraj, commemorating the coronation of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj in 1674. Inspired by his principles of democratic welfare, strategic fortitude, and ethical governance, the journal provides an inclusive forum for transformative academic discourse.

Call for Papers

We invite original research articles, review papers, and case studies for our upcoming Volume 1, Issue 2 (July–December 2026 Edition).

Author Guidelines

  • Word Limit Manuscripts should be between 6,000 and 8,000 words, including abstract and references.
  • Citation Style Follow APA 7th Edition for Social Sciences/Humanities or IEEE format for Technology/Sciences.
  • Originality Policy Strict plagiarism check via Turnitin. Similarity index must be strictly below 10%.
  • Formatting Times New Roman 12pt, 1.5 spacing. Submit as editable .docx files.
